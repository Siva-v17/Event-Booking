How booking works

From brief to curtain call

A typical booking moves through five checkpoints — each with a named owner on our side, visible to you in the dashboard.

Day 1

Brief & budget

We scope headcount, format and budget ceiling, and shortlist three venues that fit your date.

Day 3

Venue walkthrough

A producer walks the shortlisted rooms with you, live or on video, and locks the floor plan.

Day 7

Production plan

Stage, AV, catering and décor vendors are booked against your run sheet and confirmed in writing.

Day 21

Guest experience

Invitations, RSVP tracking and on-site registration are configured inside your dashboard.

Event day

Live production

A producer runs the floor from load-in to strike, with a live channel open to your team.
